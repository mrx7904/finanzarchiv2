# #39 Savings hints

**Goal:** The archive points out where money could be saved, from what it knows, without advising.
**Requirements**: FA-42 · **Depends on**: #34, #33

## Done when

- [x] Hints by code, each with its numbers and the bookings behind it: a recurring payment whose amount rose; two recurring payments of the same category at once (two streaming services); fees of banks and payment services in the year; a category clearly above the year before; a recurring payment that still runs although its party sent a cancellation (when the archive has one).
- [x] Each hint says what it observed, never what to buy, sell or invest. No hint about investments, loans or insurance cover.
- [x] The owner can dismiss a hint, and it stays dismissed until its numbers change.
- [ ] Tests with invented bookings. Measured on a copy of the owner's archive: how many hints of each kind, and a sample checked by the owner.

**Status:** done on 2026-11-27 but for the sample the owner checks (`knowledge/decision-hints.md`, "Open").
