# #35 Salary from the payslips

**Goal:** Brutto, Netto and the deductions of every month are at hand, and a missing payslip is seen.
**Requirements**: FA-39 · **Depends on**: #13

## Done when

- [ ] Brutto, Netto, Lohnsteuer, Solidaritätszuschlag, Kirchensteuer and the four social insurances per month and per year from the payslips that were filed.
- [ ] A month within the range of the archived payslips without one is "fehlt", never zero. Months before the first and after the last show nothing.
- [ ] The Netto against the booking that paid it (#24), a difference named.
- [ ] Tests with invented payslips. Measured on a copy of the owner's archive, counts only.
