# #40 The start view

**Goal:** The owner sees at first glance what waits and does it with one click.
**Requirements**: FA-43 · **Depends on**: #38, #39, #44, #45

The view follows the map and the rules of [`knowledge/decision-views.md`](../knowledge/decision-views.md), its picture is in `src/entwurf/` (#50).

## Done when

- [ ] "Start" first in the sidebar: one sentence on what waits, then "Als Nächstes", the steps most pressing first, each with one button that does it.
- [ ] Steps from the archive, derived and never stored: documents in "Prüfen", orders without an invoice, a mailbox or watched folder with a problem, an account with a gap between its statements, the local model out of reach, a savings hint.
- [ ] Beside them a few key figures of the running year (income, spending, balance), each opening its evaluation.
- [ ] Seen by the owner on the real archive.
